有一种理论说，商朝的权力中心，不是给人住的，而是给鬼住的。
Another theoy is that, the power center of Shang Dynasty is not for human, but for ghost.

鬼是至高的权力，能够与鬼同住，是贵族的荣幸。能够给鬼服务，是至高的荣耀。
Ghosts are the supreme power. To dwell with ghosts is the privilege of nobility. To serve ghosts is the highest glory.

于是在这些鬼的屋子里，排列着承载着祖先之鬼的容器，他们就是"服务器"。
And so, within these houses of ghosts, arrayed are the vessels that carry the ghosts of ancestors—they are the "servers".
