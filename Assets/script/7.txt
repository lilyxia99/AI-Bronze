召唤祖先不仅可以帮助他们，而且可以作为权力的象征、统治的手段。有能力祭祖本身就是一种科技、人力和财力的显现。
Being able to ask ancester not only helps them, but also is a symbol of power and a means of dominance. 

因为这代表你有语言和青铜器的科技，你有力量去煮人，还有比其他人更强的祖先。
It manifests that you are have the technology of language and bronze casting, you have the power to cook people, and have more powerful ancesters than anyone else. 

商朝后期，商人不再允许外族通婚，他们把邻居羌人拿来当牛羊一样作为祭祀的燃料焚烧。
In the late Shang dynasty, the Shang people no longer permitted intermarriage with foreign tribes. 
They took their neighbors, the Qiang people, and burned them as sacrificial fuel, just like cattle and sheep.

“羌”的字形上面是一只羊，下面是两只脚。对于商朝人来说，羌人和羊、牛这样的动物差不多。
The character '羌' (Qiang) consists of a sheep on top and two feet below. 
To the Shang people, the Qiang were not much different from animals such as sheep and cattle.

在商的西边住着周人，他们后来取代了商朝。
To the west of the Shang lived the Zhou people, who later overthrew the Shang dynasty.
