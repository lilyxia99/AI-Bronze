商朝人的王乐于占卜。他几乎无所不问：明天会下雨吗？今天是个打猎的好时间吗？我会有个儿子还是女儿……？
Shang's leader LOVES fortune-telling.
He asked about almost anything -- Is it gonna rain tomorrow? Is today a good day to hunt? Am I gonna have daughter or son?...

占卜的时候，巫师会在龟甲上写下文字，放在火上烤，上面显示的裂痕就是卜辞。
When doing fortune-telling, wizards wrote on the shell of turtles and burn them. The cracks becomes the signs that are delivered. 

语言，作为商朝的核心科技之一，是和祖先对话的工具。
Language, as one of their core technologies, is their tool to communicate with ancesters.
