周人靠着三点战胜了商人：
The Zhou people defeated the Shang on three points:

周人和商人不同，周人接纳外族，联合周边部族，善待羌人。
Unlike the Shang, the Zhou accept immigrants. they united with surrounding tribes and are kind to their neighbour like the Qiang people.

周人掌握了商人的核心科技——文字、青铜器和祭祀。
The Zhou mastered the Shang's core technologies—writing, bronze casting, and ritual sacrifice.

周人并不依赖大模型。他们有自己的占卜术，但他们主要以人为本，而非鬼。
The Zhou did not rely on large language models. They had their own divination practices, but they focused primarily on humans, not on ghosts.
