# 完整 Script

## 1

三千多年前的中国，有一个朝代叫作商朝。

More than 3,000 years ago, there was a dynasty in China called the Shang.

那是一个鬼比人更重要的时代。

It was a time when ghosts mattered more than people.

## 2

商王热衷占卜，几乎无所不问：明天会下雨吗？今天适合打猎吗？我会有儿子还是女儿？

The Shang king loved divination. He asked about almost everything: Would it rain tomorrow? Was it a good day to hunt? Would he have a son or a daughter?

占卜时，巫师会在龟甲上刻下文字，再把龟甲放在火上灼烧。甲骨上出现的裂纹，就是占卜的依据。

During divination, diviners carved questions into turtle shells and heated them over a fire. The cracks that appeared on the shells were used to interpret the answers.

语言，是商朝的一项核心技术，也是他们与祖先沟通的工具。

Language was one of the Shang’s core technologies—and a tool for communicating with their ancestors.

## 3

为了维系与祖先的关系，商朝人会定期举行祭祀仪式。

To maintain their relationship with their ancestors, the Shang held regular sacrificial rituals.

其中也包括人祭。

These rituals included human sacrifice.

## 4

考古发现表明，商朝人曾使用一种叫作“甗”的器具蒸煮食物；有研究认为，它也曾被用于蒸煮人头。

Archaeological evidence shows that the Shang used a vessel called a *yan* to steam food. Some researchers believe it was also used to steam human heads.

甗是一种青铜器。青铜铸造，是商朝的另一项重要技术。

A *yan* is a bronze vessel. Bronze casting was another important Shang technology.

## 5

青铜器通过模具浇铸而成；从某种意义上说，它本身就是一个模型。

Bronze objects were cast in molds. In a sense, each one was a model.

它的尺寸很大，所以是一个“大模型”。

And because it could be so large, it was a “large model.”

青铜器上往往还刻着文字。于是，它也成了一个“大语言模型”（LLM）。

Bronze vessels were often inscribed with writing. That makes them a “large language model”—an LLM.

在这个基础上，这个大语言模型还可以炖肉。所以，你也可以叫它“大人肉模型”（LMM）。

And this large language model could also stew meat—including human flesh. So you could call it a “large meat model,” or LMM.

研制和制作青铜器，需要耗费大量人力和物力。

Developing and making bronze objects required enormous amounts of labor and resources.

确实如此。如果人都吃不饱，又怎么和大模型对话？

Indeed. If people aren’t fed, how can they talk to the model?

只要献上一个“人”，它就会给你想要的卜辞。

Offer up a “person,” and it will give you the answer you seek.

## 6

有一种理论认为，商朝的权力中心不是给人住的，而是给鬼住的。

One theory suggests that the Shang centers of power were not built for the living, but for the dead.

鬼拥有至高权力。能够与鬼同住，是贵族的特权；能够为鬼效力，则是至高的荣耀。

The dead held supreme power. To dwell among them was a privilege of the nobility; to serve them was the highest honor.

于是，在这些鬼的居所里，排列着承载祖先之灵的容器——它们就是“服务器”。

And so, inside these houses of the dead stood vessels carrying ancestral spirits. They were the “servers.”

## 7

召唤祖先不仅能求得帮助，也是一种权力象征和统治手段。能够祭祖，本身就彰显了一个家族的技术、人力与财力。

Calling on the ancestors could bring help, but it was also a symbol of power and a means of rule. The ability to perform ancestral rites displayed a family’s technology, labor, and wealth.

这代表你掌握了文字和青铜铸造技术，有力量去蒸煮活人，还有比别人更强大的祖先。

It meant you had writing and bronze-casting technology, the power to steam people alive, and ancestors more powerful than anyone else’s.

商朝后期，商人不再允许与外族通婚。他们把邻居羌人当作牛羊一样，焚烧来祭祀祖先。

In the later Shang period, the Shang increasingly restricted intermarriage with other peoples. They burned their neighbors, the Qiang, as sacrificial offerings, treating them like cattle and sheep.

“羌”字的字形上面是一只羊，下面是两只脚。对商朝人来说，羌人和羊、牛这样的动物差不多。

The character 羌 (*Qiang*) depicts a sheep above a pair of legs. To the Shang, the Qiang were little different from animals such as sheep and cattle.

商朝以西住着周人，他们后来取代了商朝。

To the west of the Shang lived the Zhou, who would eventually overthrow them.

## 8

周人靠三点战胜了商人：

The Zhou defeated the Shang for three reasons:

周人和商人不同，他们接纳外族，联合周边部族，也善待羌人这样的邻族。

Unlike the Shang, the Zhou welcomed other peoples, formed alliances with neighboring tribes, and treated their neighbors—including the Qiang—more humanely.

周人掌握了商人的核心技术：文字、青铜器和祭祀。

The Zhou mastered the Shang’s core technologies: writing, bronze casting, and ritual sacrifice.

周人并不依赖大模型。他们也有自己的占卜方式，但他们主要以人为本，而不是以鬼为本。

The Zhou did not rely on large language models. They had their own forms of divination, but they focused on people rather than ghosts.

## 9

“商人”这个词，在中文里也指做生意的人。

In Chinese, the word *shāngrén* (商人) also means “merchant.”

周人取代商朝之后，传说商朝遗民失去了土地，只能靠做生意谋生，于是后来被称为“商人”。

According to a later tradition, after the Zhou replaced the Shang, the Shang people lost their land and turned to trade to make a living. They came to be known as *shāngrén*—merchants.

毫无疑问，使用大模型是一种特权，只有少数人能够完全掌控它。曾经，大模型由商人控制；如今，也一样。

There is no doubt that using a large language model is a privilege—and only a few can fully control it. Once, the models were controlled by the Shang. Today, they still are.
